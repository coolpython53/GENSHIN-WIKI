# Clearwater Jade

image::https://raw.githubusercontent.com/coolpython53/GENSHIN-WIKI/main/pages/Items/Materials/Local_Specialties/Clearwater_Jade/blueprint.jpg

Clearwater Jade is a Local Specialty found in Chenyu Vale, Liyue.

## How to Obtain
Clearwater Jade can be obtained in Chenyu Vale, either growing freely or on the shells of Jadestone Turtles.

## Shops
There is 1 NPC that sells Clearwater Jade:
- Fengtai (cost: 1,000{[Mora]page::Currencies/Mora}) 5 in stock
   - Refreshes every 3 Days

## Crafting
No recipes use Clearwater Jade as an ingredient.
