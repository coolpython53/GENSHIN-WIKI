# Cecilia

image::https://raw.githubusercontent.com/coolpython53/GENSHIN-WIKI/main/pages/Items/Materials/Local_Specialties/Cecilia/Cecilia.jpg

Cecilias are a Local Specialty of Mondstadt found in the wild on Starsnatch Cliff.

## How to Obtain
Cecilias grow exclusively on Starsnatch Cliff.
Additionally, the following NPCs give Cecilias after following certain dialogue branches for the first time:
- Grace in the City of Mondstadt, Mondstadt (3 Cecilias)
- Sharp on Starsnatch Cliff, Mondstadt (5 Cecilias)

## Shops
There is 1 Shop that sells Cecilia:
- Flora (Cost: 1,000 {[Mora]page::Currencies/Mora}) 5 in stock
  - Refreshes every 3 days

## Crafting
There is 1 item that can be crafted using Cecilia:
- Anemoculus Resonance Stone
  - Dandelion Seed ×5
  - Cecilia ×5
  - Crystal Chunk ×1
  - Mora ×500
