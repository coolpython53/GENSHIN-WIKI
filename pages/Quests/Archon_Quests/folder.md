# Archon Quests
Archon Quests are Quests that tell the main story of the Traveler and Paimon as they travel the world of Teyvat in search of the Traveler's lost sibling. Players need to reach a certain Adventure Rank (AR) to unlock each Act in this storyline.

The difficulty of enemies in Archon Quests as well as the level of trial characters (up to 90) is calculated based on current World Level.
